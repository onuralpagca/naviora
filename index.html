Privacy Policy

Naviora Surf · Effective 2 August 2026

Naviora Surf collects no data. None at all. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no account, and the app makes no network connections of any kind.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the iOS app Naviora Surf, published by Hasan Onuralp Agca. In this document, "the app" means that app and "we" means the developer.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. To be specific:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any other contact detail.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers or your IP address.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not collect usage or analytics data, and there is no crash-reporting service.
  • We do not use cookies, pixels, fingerprinting or any other tracking technology.
  • There is no account, login or sign-up, so nothing about you is ever identified.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs — no advertising network, no analytics provider, no attribution or measurement partner, no social login. Nothing in the app sends information anywhere, so there is nothing to share and no one to share it with.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress is saved locally using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. It stays on your device, is never transmitted, and is not readable by us or anyone else.

Stored locallyPurpose
Levels completed and stars earnedContinue where you left off
Shell balanceBuy boats and oceans in the shop
Equipped boat and oceanRemember your choices
Best Endless score and distanceShow your personal best
Sound, effects and haptics switchesRemember your settings

Deleting the app removes all of it permanently. Because none of it leaves the device, it does not transfer to a new phone.

Network access

The app does not connect to the internet. It works fully offline, including in airplane mode. It requests no network permission and opens no connections.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13. There is no advertising, no chat, no user-generated content, no external links and no way to contact other players. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing that could be collected from a child.

Purchases

The app is free and contains no in-app purchases. Boats and oceans are earned by playing. We never see or handle payment information of any kind.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, deletion and portability under the GDPR, the CCPA and similar laws all concern data a company holds about you. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to request, export or erase. If you want your local game progress removed, delete the app.

Data provided to us by Apple

Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated App Store statistics such as download and territory counts. That is collected and controlled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by this app, and it never identifies an individual person.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, the change will be described here, and the effective date at the top will change.
